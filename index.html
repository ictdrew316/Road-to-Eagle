Eagle rank currently requires 13 merit badges (10 fixed + one each from three choose‑one groups) toward 21 total. Citizenship in Society was retired as an Eagle‑required badge on Feb 27, 2026, dropping the required count from 14 to 13 — this tracker reflects that current rule.
